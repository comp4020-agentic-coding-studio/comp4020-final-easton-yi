# Crit 8 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was finding a concrete purpose for real-time collaboration.
I proposed a shared wooden-tower scene in which I can see another person's
held stick before either of us releases it, so I can adapt my own placement to
what they are about to do. That made collaboration part of the building
decisions themselves, rather than a feature added to satisfy a checklist.

This is still a design intention. Whether seeing a partner's held stick
actually changes how people place their own is something I haven't yet
observed with real people.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to become a developer who takes responsibility for defining the
experience and for deciding what counts as good. Before any implementation, I
asked for the idea to be turned into explicit rules, constraints and
acceptance criteria. That gave me something to assess the agent's work
against that means more than whether the code runs: I can ask whether it does
what I decided the experience should be.
