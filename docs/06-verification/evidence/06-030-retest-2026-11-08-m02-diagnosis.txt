== Symptom (2026-11-07, unmodified spec)
At a sender's 21st-24th placement Playwright waited 5 minutes for Place to be enabled; the serial suite then skipped
M05, M08, M09, M10 and L03. This tree failed 3 of 10 runs, the clean fa23e6a build 1 of 9 (06-020, M02 section).

== Every condition that disables Place (src/client/workshop/Workshop.tsx:874)
  disabled = !canEdit || phase !== "draft" || validityText(validity, phase).tone === "bad"
  canEdit  = room.live && room.lease && room.mode === "build" && connStatus === "open" && !ended   (line 179)

  validity : validate(draft, sticks' latest authoritative poses, table, bounds) - re-run on every world.frame,

  tone bad : intersecting, out of bounds, capacity, invalid. place() also returns without sending unless the

== Captured transitions (minimal recorder; 06-031)
Every placement in the failing run: Place enabled with 'Ready to place · drops 0.10 u', then 'Waiting for server',
then idle. At the stuck placement: Add -> 'Ready to place · drops 0.10 u' (Place enabled) -> 'drops 0.11 u' ->
about 14 ms later '✕ Intersecting; adjust position' with Place disabled, Add disabled (phase draft), connection
Connected; no further transition for the 30 s window. No place command was sent for it.

== Cause
Product behaviour is as designed and already specified by M05: a held stick is added 0.1 u above the surface
below it; while the stack under it is still settling (partners' sticks placed 0.7 s apart on a tall stack), a
settling stick comes to rest inside the held stick; the per-frame re-check marks it intersecting and disables
Place; nothing re-enables it without the person's input. canEdit, the lease, the connection and the command
state were all fine - the refusal is the client's advisory check doing its job, before anything is sent.
Test defect: placeVia clicked Place and waited for it to become enabled, a state only a person's action (move the
stick, or cancel and add again, as the message says) can produce. A second form of the same cause: the click
lands just as the stick turns, place() re-checks, sends nothing, and placeVia returned no stick (seen 2026-11-08 in
plan 2, P1 run 6: M09 failed with A's draft.start but no command.place in the server log).
Where it happens: all 11 re-adds recorded in M02 (plans 2 and 3) were at placements 23-25 (A 23 x2, B 24 x6, C 25 x3), the top
of the 26-stick stack - the same positions as the 2026-10-12 finding F2 (run 1 #24 B, run 2 #23 A, run 4 #24 B).

== Why the diagnostic copy passed 10/10 on 2026-11-07
Not established. The refusal occurs in about half of the fixed runs (plan 3: 9 of 20 runs), yet the diagnostic
copy, which added an isEnabled() round trip before each click, never met it in 10 runs; its extra round trip may
have let the stack settle before Add, but this was not measured.

== Fix (tests/e2e/crit9-multiplayer.spec.ts only; no product change)
placeVia keeps its normal Add/Place actions. Only when the draft shows exactly '✕ Intersecting; adjust position'
(tone bad) and no place command was sent - either Place stayed disabled for its normal 2 s actionability wait, or
the click was refused before sending - it does what the message asks: Escape (cancel) and Add again. At most 2
re-adds per placement; each is recorded (Placed.readded; observations.timing.readded). Any other refusal fails.
Nothing force-enables Place, bypasses validation, changes rate limits or adds a blind retry.

== Regression check: M05b (new, in the same serial suite, before M08)
On a fresh work with an empty table: B adds a stick; A commits a stick lying flat on the table under it (second
socket, as M05). Asserts: B's draft reads Intersecting with Place disabled; still so after the room reports saved
(world still); a plain Place click (the old helper's action) times out; no place command sent; placeVia then
re-adds exactly once and places; server and both pages agree on exactly the two sticks.
Mutation (old plain click restored, scratch copy): M05b hangs until the test timeout - logs/m02-fix-mutant2.
