Before the fix, 'Return to nearest safe point' teleported to the recorded point: above the terrain it left the explorer
flying (jumpTo kept height > ground + 1 as flight); inside the dam's body it put the capsule inside collision.

Fix (src/client/world/WorldView.ts, src/client/world/WorldStage.tsx): recovery calls jumpTo(sp, { land: true }), which
stands on the loaded surface under the point and is never left flying; nearestSafePoint() tries the nearest six and skips
one whose stood capsule is blocked. World data, landmark recipes and the manifest are unchanged. Viewpoints (Go to a
landmark) are not changed: a ground viewpoint above the terrain still arrives flying (open; 10-02).
