I found and fixed the slow-settling defect (for new works only), traced the reconnect-indicator failure to the test's disconnect method rather than the app, and moved the build-dependent test suite to its own command.

1. Root causes

Slow settling and blocked saving: a real defect, fixed for new works.
- Cause: the physics engine handles the round centre of the table badly. A stick resting near the edge on one of the four diagonals sinks about 0.28 u into the table and keeps vibrating at about 1 u/s indefinitely.
- Why saving was blocked: the "settled" check correctly sees that motion. Versions, and with them exhibits and push, wait for a settled structure, so they waited forever.
- What it wasn't: not the save rule, not a step-scheduling problem in the test environment, not a measurement error, and not cured by more solver iterations.
- How it was found: I rebuilt the browser's building sequence offline with the server's own code, at each run's recorded click times. The recorded schedules alone settled normally, but slightly varied timings left 5 of 20 never settling and 6 more taking 24–112 s. Reconnect indicator: the test's disconnect method, not the app.
- In 20 instrumented disconnects, "Reconnecting…" appeared within 1.6 ms of the socket actually closing, evetime.
- What failed was Playwright's offline mode. When the page closed its socket while offline, the close sometimes stalled and no close event ever fired, until offline was switched off. I reproduced this once inattempts while the server was streamin0 with an idle room.
- The original check timed from asking the socket to close, not from it closing.
- Also noted: Playwright never fires the browser's online event, so reconnects in that method only happened on the client's backoff timer. Real browsers fire it.
